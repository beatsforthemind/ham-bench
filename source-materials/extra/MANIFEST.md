### Topic callout

Counts below are usable JSON questions whose stem or answers contain the word. A question can fall in more than one topic.

**E5 Electrical principles** — 4 exam questions, 4 groups, 49 usable.

- Resonance: E5A, “Resonance and Q” (series and parallel resonance, Q, half-power bandwidth). 12 questions: E5A01–E5A04 and E5A06–E5A13. Also E5D06 and E5D07.
- Reactance: E5B, “phase angle in reactive circuits and components; admittance and susceptance” (E5B05, E5B12). Also E5A01, E5A03, E5A04, E5A09; E5C01, E5C03, E5C06, E5C08; E5D02, E5D07, E5D11.
- Impedance: concentrated in E5C, “Coordinate systems and phasors” (E5C02, E5C05–E5C07, E5C09–E5C12), which uses Figure E5-1. Also E5A03–E5A05, E5B03, E5B06, E5B12, E5D07.
- Figure E5-1 is the rectangular-coordinate plot for E5C10–E5C12.

**E6 Components** — 6 exam questions, 6 groups, 68 usable. E6D07 is deleted; 11 questions remain in E6D, “Inductors and piezoelectricity.” Resonance appears in E6D02. Figures: E6-1 on E6A10–E6A11, E6-2 on E6B10, E6-3 on E6C08, E6C10, and E6C11. Decibel notation appears in E6E05 (noise figure, dB and dBm).

**E7 Circuits** — 8 exam questions, 8 groups, 99 usable.

- Filters: E7C, “Filters and matching networks” (E7C01, E7C05, E7C06, E7C08–E7C11). Also E7F digital/DSP filters (E7F02, E7F03, E7F09, E7F12–E7F14), E7D supply filtering, and E7G02.
- Op-amps: E7G, “Operational amplifiers: characteristics and applications.” Op-amp wording in E7G01, E7G03–E7G06, E7G08, E7G12. Figure E7-3 is the op-amp circuit used by E7G02, E7G07, E7G09, E7G10, and E7G11. Other mentions: E7C05, E7A10, E7F14, and E6C07.
- Reactance modulators: E7E, “reactance, phase, and balanced modulators” (E7E01, E7E02, E7E04, E7E10).
- Impedance matching is named in the E7C group line; E7C04 uses “impedance”. E7G01, E7G03, and E7G12 use “impedance”.

**E8 Signals** — 4 exam questions, 4 groups, 48 usable. E8A Fourier analysis, RMS, PEP, and analog/digital conversion; E8B modulation index, deviation ratio, and multiplexing including OFDM; E8C digital modes, information rate versus bandwidth, and constellation diagrams; E8D keying defects, overmodulation, digital codes, and spread spectrum. E8D09 uses dB for an IMD level. Filter wording also appears in E8A10 and E8D05. E8D11 mentions a transmission line.

**E9 Antennas and transmission lines** — 8 exam questions, 8 groups, 93 usable. E9E10 is deleted.

- Impedance: E9E, “Impedance matching” (10 usable: E9E04–E9E08). Also E9F01, E9F02, E9F04, E9F05, E9F09–E9F12 and the E9G Smith-chart questions.
- Transmission lines: E9F, all 12 questions (open and shorted feed lines, coax versus open wire, velocity factor, electrical length, coaxial dielectrics, microstrip). Feed-line or transmission-line wording also in E9A, E9E, and E9G.
- Smith chart: E9G, “The Smith chart”, all 11 questions (E9G01–E9G11). Figure E9-3 is used by E9G06 and E9G07. Reactance wording in E9G02, E9G04, E9G06–E9G08, and E9G10.
- Decibels: E9A ERP/EIRP and gain (E9A02, E9A05–E9A07, E9A12, including dBd and dBi) and E9B patterns (E9B01–E9B04, E9B07), plus E9D01. Figures E9-1 (E9B01–E9B03) and E9-2 (E9B04–E9B06).

**Decibels, whole pool.** The word “decibel” does not occur in the JSON. The units dB, dBm, dBi, or dBd occur in **25** usable questions: E1B01, E1C10, E1F03, E1F11, E4C04–E4C06, E4D01, E4D10, E4D12–E4D14, E6E05, E8D09, E9A02, E9A05–E9A07, E9A12, E9B01–E9B04, E9B07, E9D01. The calculation-heavy groups are E4C/E4D (noise floor, dynamic range, link margin; E4D12 and E4D13 are the 10 W wording from the January 31, 2024 errata) and E9A/E9B (antenna gain and pattern ratios).

**E0 Safety** — 1 exam question, 1 group, 12 usable (E0A01–E0A12). Syllabus: “RF radiation hazards; hazardous materials; grounding.” Stems cover station grounding, RF exposure evaluation, MPE and SAR, and tower fall protection (100% tie-off and lanyards).
