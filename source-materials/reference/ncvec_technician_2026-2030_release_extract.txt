2026-2030 Technician Question Pool Public Release

Errata Issued February 19, 2026

The following changes must be made to the originally posted Technician Pool

In the pool itself, 4 questions modified:

T1C01 – change the question to read:

For which classes of amateur radio licenses does the FCC currently issue new licenses?

T5A05 – change the question to read:

A difference in which of the following causes electron flow?

T7A09 – change the question to read:

What is the function of the switch which selects either SSB or CW-FM on some VHF power amplifiers?

T0A10 – change the question to read:

What hazard exists when rapidly charging or discharging an unprotected battery?
